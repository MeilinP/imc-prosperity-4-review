# Manual track — source screenshots

The exchange returns a downloadable log for the algorithmic track and nothing but a results
page for the manual puzzles. These screenshots are therefore the only surviving record of what
was submitted and what it scored, and every figure in
[`manual/README.md`](../README.md) traces back to one of them.

They were scattered across three separate folders, none of them complete; collected here so the
record is in one place and under version control.

| | files | what they show |
|---|---:|---|
| `round1/` | 5 | both order books with their resale terms, the submitted bids, and the PnL-versus-volume curves the platform published afterwards |
| `round2/` | 4 | the qualifying total, and the three-pillar breakdown with the realised hit rate and speed rank |
| `round3/` | 4 | the round's detailed results (algorithmic and manual scored separately, with ranks) and the distribution of every team's two bids |
| `round4/` | 4 | the twelve-contract panel with the submitted side and volume on each leg |
| `round5/` | 3 | the nine-good allocation with investment and fee per position |

Round 3's field distribution is the most useful single image: it gives the realised average
second bid (859), which is the quantity the whole round turns on and which no amount of
pre-round analysis could have observed.
